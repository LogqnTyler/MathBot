\paragraph*{GOAL:} Historically, over {\bf 98\%} of MATH 1190/1210 students make mistakes on Knowledge Checkpoint 1 in any given semester. So, if you made mistakes on Knowledge Checkpoint 1, then please know you are not alone!\\

{\bf Being honest with yourself when you respond to these prompts is a critically important for your growth.} This can be difficult, but please do your best!\\
