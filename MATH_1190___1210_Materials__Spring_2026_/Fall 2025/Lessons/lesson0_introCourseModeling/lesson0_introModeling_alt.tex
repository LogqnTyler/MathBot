{\bf How much  CO$_2$ is in the atmosphere in the year 2025?}
\enum{
\item Let's start with some intuition.  What's your best guess for the amount of CO$_2$ in the atmosphere in the year 2025?  
\vs{2}
\item On a scale of $0-100$, how confident are you in your estimate? 
$0$  = ``not at all confident" and $100=$``$100\%$ confident."
\newpage
\item What decisions did you make and/or questions did you have when you made your best guess? 

\vfill

\item Work together to brainstorm at least two strategies to refine your estimate and/or increase your confidence in your estimate. You might find it helpful to use Desmos widget ``First Day of Class: The Keeling Curve"  to implement your ideas. This can be found on the ToDo tab at \url{https://student.desmos.com} using code \desmosClassCode (You might need to create a free account at Amplify). 

\vfill

\item In what ways would you describe how the amount of CO$_2$ is changing each year?
\vfill

\item What other information might be helpful to further improve your estimate and/or build confidence in your estimate? What mathematical tools might be helpful?

\vfill
